Shambled City (Level 1-3) [2]
The city used to be called Smiles City, one that is filled with vibrancy and life. Tourists around the star system love to come to Smiles City for a visit.

Ever since the rise of Lord Eego and his troops, visitors and denizens alike have isolated the city. The aptly renaming of the city as Shambled City reflected the change that has been going through on the planet.

Lord Eego himself claims the useless city today. His men are sometimes found looting in the abandoned shopping complexes of the area.
