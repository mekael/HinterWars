Gallant City (Level 1-3)
The dysfunctional Gallant City is the result of the Eegos starting their first nuclear warfare. The city population of more than two million was wiped out with a single blast.

Shocked denizens of Cacto were quick to turn caves into thriving underground towns. Forces to maintain peace and order were setup, to fight against the opposing forces.

The city itself stands as a disappointing testimony that the human war history will repeat itself once again.
