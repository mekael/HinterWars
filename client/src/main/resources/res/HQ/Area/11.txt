Ruins of Ar'nede
Ar'nede discovered this area right after his team started excavation work on the Alpha excavation site. Further exploration along this area was never done due to the commitment required to work on the first site. Looters frequent this site to search for valuables. Taikaan authorities has started encouraging adventurers to frequent the area to keep looters away, in an attempt to preserve whatever is left on the site.

Recommended Level 7 - 10
