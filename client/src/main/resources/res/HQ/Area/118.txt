Outskirts (Level 1-3)
An ordinary forest lying near a legendary cult ruin. Legends say that the unacceptables from the Taikuus, Humans, Firans and Brucians come together to form a cult. While the cult has long being wiped out, the ruins that exist here deep down in the tunnels of Crapucus are definitely made of materials not of this world. Some say they are made of 'the One'.
