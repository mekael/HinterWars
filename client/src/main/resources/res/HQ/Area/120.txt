Abandoned Production Plant (Level 1-3)
This complex used to be producing magic dust that the Brucians demanded. Ever since someone tried to steal the secret of how the magic dust is produced, the whole facility is destroyed and vacated.

No one is really sure where the new plant is situated. Yet, left in this area is a shroud of energy, which did not manage to disperse after all these years. These seem to be the only clues left behind as to how the production process might be.
