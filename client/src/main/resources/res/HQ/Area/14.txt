Terran Scrapyard
The junkyard has some historical significance to the humans. The junkyard used to be a scrap yard of some sort. Things that were meant to be recycled were stored at the location. Unfortunately, the "promised" recycling technology never came to fruition fast enough. Overtime, the backlog of things stored in the scrap yard became to be known as junk. It now resembles more of a landfill dumpsite then to a scrap yard. Although the "promised" recycling technology has finally come of age, it will still take quire a few years to clear that entire backlog. There just is not enough recycling capacity to clear the backlog.

Recommended Level 7 - 10
