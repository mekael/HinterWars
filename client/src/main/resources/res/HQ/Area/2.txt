Schloza Crystal Mines
This is one of the biggest crystal mining facility on Fira. The mine used to be a natural cave until mining started. Most of its vast network of tunnels was there before crystal mining started. Some more tunnels had been added by the present miners since. However, a large portion of the tunnel network remains unexplored due to its overwhelming size. The authority were not certain how the caves came about, but are fairly certain that vicious creatures lie in wait in its unexplored depths. Let all adventurers who dare enter the unexplored area beware, and be prepared.

Recommended level 4 - 6
