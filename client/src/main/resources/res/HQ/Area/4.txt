Oasis of Schaken
Many oases dot the desert landscape of Fira. The cool clean puddles of water just seem to bubble out of the ground at random; supplying the arid area with some much needed moisture. Some of these oases seem to move around abit. This particular oasis was discovered by one of the earlier Firan hunter Schaken, hence the name. Its situated not too far from the colony. The colony was built with consideration to its proximity to the oasis. The Oasis of Schaken harbors a very deep pool of fresh water that seems to replenish itself quite regularly. It is highly unlikely that this would be one of the shallow shifting oasis then dot most of the landscape. The oasis makes decent hunting grounds due to the plethora of Firan wildlife it attracts. Also one of the reasons why the colony was situated a certain distance away from the oasis, for safety. 

Recommended Level 7 - 10
