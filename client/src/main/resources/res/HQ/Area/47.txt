Valley of Death  (Level 24-26)
All the goods to be transported from the warehouse to the spaceport are required to pass through the perilous valley. It is rumoured that many Firans who entered the valley fail to return to their homes. Some even said that gigantic man-eating plants have been the caused of the missing Firans, found populated along the valley.

While no one knows for sure if this has been all true or just a myth, they continue to use this valley as the major route to transport their goods.
