The Vormix Asylum: Jail Level (Level 11-13)
Flirting with telepathic, psychic and ethereal planes is not all smooth. Some Taikuus has taken such studies to an extreme that they have gone mad, and hence banished into the ethereal forever.

Jail Level contains Crazy Taikuus and Metamorphed Taikuus who have their soul trapped with the elements in the ethereal plane.
