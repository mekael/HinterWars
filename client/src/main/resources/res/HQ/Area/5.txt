Jungles of Brucana
Brucana is covered literally with forest as far as the eye can see. It has only 1 continent, which the Brucians call home, and the rest of the planet is covered with fresh water. The deep canopies of leaves in Brucana hid not only the Brucians, but also the beasts of Brucana.

Recommended Level 1 - 3
