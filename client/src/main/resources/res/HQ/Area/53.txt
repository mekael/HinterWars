Cult Ruins: Chamber of Mummified (Level 24-26)
Legends say that the unacceptables from the Taikuus, Humans, Firans and Brucians come together to form a cult. While the cult has long being wiped out, the ruins that exist here deep down in the tunnels of Crapucus are definitely made of materials not of this world. Some say they are made of 'the One'.

Across the bridge lies the Chamber of Mummified. It is believed that offerings were made to 'the One' through this manner.
