Mother Tree Glade
The Mother Tree Glade is place where the Mother Tree of Brucana grows. It gives births to new baby Brucians after a Brucian performs the fertility rites. Brucians patrol this place from time to time in preparation for any unsuspecting new Brucians being born. In the early days, new hopeful fathers would often find the bloody remains of the empty fruit of the Mother Tree torn open; the work of the wildlife in the vicinity. It was very sad, but it's a true story.

Recommended Level 4 - 6
