Vegetable Farm of Saramar (Level 31-33)
Sadhulor functions primarily as a farming community because of the excellent soil it is blessed with among all the planets the Human has colonised.

While farming is not where Humans excel in, with the help of fellow Brucians, they were able to learn the art of farming.

Vegetable Farm of Saramar, representing the 'Sa' in Sadhulor uses hydro-phonic technologies as well as the minerals from the soils of Sadhulor to grow one of the best vegetables that can be found in the star system.
