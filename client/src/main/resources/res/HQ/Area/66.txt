Wood Plantation of D' Humil (Level 34-36)
Sadhulor functions primarily as a farming community because of the excellent soil it is blessed with among all the planets the Human has colonised.

While farming is not where Humans excel in, with the help of fellow Brucians, they were able to learn the art of farming.

Wood Plantation of D' Humil represents the 'Dhu' in Sadhulor, grows a varieties of trees in its plantation from rubber to cotton. A tree from a seedling can be grown to full-blown in a short 18 to 21 months. Under such growing speed, the Humans no longer have to worry about possible deforestation that happens back at Old Earth.
