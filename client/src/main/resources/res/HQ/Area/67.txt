Wine Farm of Lornasia (Level 37-40)
Sadhulor functions primarily as a farming community because of the excellent soil it is blessed with among all the planets the Human has colonised.

While farming is not where Humans excel in, with the help of fellow Brucians, they were able to learn the art of farming.

Wine Farm of Lornasia represents the 'Lor' in Sadhulor, produced one of the finest wines that the star system can offer. Lornasia also host the annual Wine Festival, attracting many Humans, Brucians, Taikuus and Firans to enjoy some peaceful time with their wine.
