Taikuus Academy: Ethereal Plane (Level 44-46)
A Taikuus cadet can spend most of his time studying about the telepathic energy and psychic ability they posses. Hence, the Plane of Telepathic Channeling and Research Facilities (the Taikuus nicknamed it 'Ethereal Plane' for convenience), has been setup in the ethereal plane for them to do their research and self-studies.

Supplying the crystals for their studies is of course, the Firans, who are their partners in the crystal trade business.
