Icex Gauntlet (Level 51-53)
The Icex Gauntlet traces back to Firan ancient times as an entertainment site to the Firans. Barbaric as it may seems, prisoners were often thrown into the gauntlet with monster and they were left to their pitiful death with millions of pairs of eyes watching them.

The Gauntlet now serves as the perfect gate for the Firan new military facility in Icex, as they prepare for an unknown war.
