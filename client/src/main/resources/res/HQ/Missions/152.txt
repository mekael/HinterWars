Ruined City of Mist 1
In ancient times, Taikuus lived on the planetary surface of Taikaan. Overtime, we outgrew all the available flat land space available on the planet. Some enterprising Taikuus tried to level mountain tops to build cities on the flat plains created. Little is known about the progression from those cities to the present day floating colonies. We have chanced upon such a city which we have name the Ruined City of Mist. Your help is needed for you to keep the area free of looters while we assemble a team for research at the ruins. There are other teams already there helping out to keep looters at bay. You will be one of the many we have recruited.
