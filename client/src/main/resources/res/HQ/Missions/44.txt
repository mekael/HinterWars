Bounty Hunt I
There was a report that one of Ecchocorp's Lieutenant  Henry,  was spotted in Terra. See if you can hunt him down. Ecchocorp has been known to be the one controlling the strings in the black markets of Xsidius. No one could pin any wrong doings on them.  Thus they have been wandering free throughout the star system, going about their bussiness. If you can take out Henry, he is also worth some bounty.
