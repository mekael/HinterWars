Firan Listening post 1

Even though their outpost hasn't been completed yet they have already started monitoring Aterian communications with the help of Terran inventions. I have been informed that they had intercepted their first message but am unable to transmit it to us due to some sensitive issues. I need you to go collect that information from them, and kill anything that gets in your way of accomplishing this mission. They said that this message was quite important.
