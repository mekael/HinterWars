Sandie's Bounty
There was once a cute baby sand bat found within one of the old Firan ruins. It was a popular tourist spot. The guides called it Sandie. Now, Sandie is all grown up and its not cute anymore. It had become a menace. His antics include frightening tourist, and attacking them for the food they carry. More bats have joined Sandie's little bat mafia. Enough is enough, the cute little Sandie is dead, the new Sandie is a menace. Time to take it down. You might want to find some friends or brings lots of potions to help you on this one.
