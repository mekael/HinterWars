1. Buildings|1. Entering Buildings>There are 7 buildings within each colony, you may enter each building by stepping on the squares located in front of each building and selecting "enter". |2. Space Port>The space port is used by the inhabitants of Xsidus as a means of transport between other planets and colonies. |3. Hospital>The hospital provides facilities to mend your wounds and energy loss sustained during battle. Also available for purchase are curative potions which gives you a way to heal yourself in battle. |4. Shop>Shops allow you to upgrade your character by purchasing items such as weapons and armor. The shop also provides a storage service, which you may use to store your items. |5. Cafe>Speak with the patrons of the cafe while you are there, some require favours and will reward you for your troubles. |5. HQ>The HQ grants you access to hunting grounds where you may train, or gather a group of friends and go on quests. |6. Arcade>Take a break once in awhile and drop by the arcade for single player mini games. |7. Stadium>The stadium is where the inhabitants of Xsidus gather to challenge one other and prove their worth. You may challenge other players in combat here once you feel strong enough. 
2. Chat|1. Sending a message>Type your message in the bottom left hand textbox and click on send, the message will be displayed in the chat bubble above your head and will be visible to other players. |2. Sending a private message>If you want to say "hello" to a friend named "ivan" type "/w ivan hello" and click on send. 
3. Buddy List|1. Add a friend>Click on the buddy list icon found at the bottom right of the screen (4th icon from the right. Type your friend's user name in the text box and click add. Do note that your friend has to be online to authorize you. 
4. Trading|How to trade>Click on the player list icon in the bottom right of the screen (3rd icon from the right). Select the player you wish to trade with and click on the trade button. 
5. Inventory|1. Checking your inventory>Click on the inventory icon found at the bottom right of the screen(Last icon from the right). You may find out more aobut an item by placing your mouse over it. 
6. Statistics|1. Character Statistics>Here you may find information regarding, skills you have learnt and may learn, level, HP,  Energy, AP, XP and XP Debt. |2. Levelling up>Head to the HQ and choose an area to explore. Engage in combat to gain XP and level up. Escape squares are available in each explorable area so you may exit whenever you wish. 
7. Quest|1. What is a quest ?>A quest consists of either 1 or many missions. You may find out the current quests you are attempting by accessing your quest log. |2. How do i get a quest ?>You my obtain quests by entering the cafe and speaking to the NPCs there. 
8. Mission|1. What is a mission ?>A mission is a task you have to perform to complete your quest. This could be as simple as speaking to an NPC, or fighting monsters. |2. Combat Missions>A mission, which requires you to engage in battle, may be accessed by entering the HQ and clicking on the "Quests" tab. |3. Bringing Friends>You may invite up to 3 other players on a combat mission after selecting the mission you wish to attempt. Once all the invited players have accepted your invitation and "readied" themselves, you can begin the mission
9. Combat|1. Turn Based Combat>Combat in Hinterwars is turned-based, meaning that each participant in the battle, will take turns to make their moves.
