An alien being crash landed on this planet in his spaceship when the Nazrus were still living here. It brought with him an object of great evil. He raised an army of fellow Nazrus to wage a war against their own brethrens. The untainted Nazrus lost the war. They retreated into hiding to become the Resistants of the newfound empire. Eventually, the empire consumed itself and it began to crumble from within. The rebels struck at the being who brought the evil object. They managed to mummify him alive, and entombed him within his own shrine. The shrine was built, initially, to honor him, but now it has become its tomb. There were no indication to what the spider like creatures were. Nothing was mentioned about them in the inscriptions. The carvings also said that he managed only to teach one other Nazru how to create the evil object. That Nazru was no where to be found after.
