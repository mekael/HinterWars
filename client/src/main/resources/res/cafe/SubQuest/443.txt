Hello again! I have a story to share with you about the Ruins of Ash'ra. Its quite an interesting story actually.  The ancient Taikuus were searching for a way to make more safe liveable space. At first they thought slicing out mountains tops to flip them over was a good idea. But they still needed more space. The current methods left the Taikuus 'high and dry' in a manner of speaking. A young Taikuus decided that he was going to make the cities fly 'high', and yet at the same time not being 'dry'.  Many explosions later, the Levistone was created. These are whats keeping the colonies afloat. The stones produce water as a by-product of the levitation process. Unfortunately for all, in one of the explosions he accidentally summoned an extra-dimensional creature into this plane of existence. This creature lives in the chasm deep beneath the city ruins. I need you to venture into the depths of the ruins to verify the truth to this story. Remember to stock up for this trip.
