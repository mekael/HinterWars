Shortage of water has led us to look for alternative ways of acquiring freshwater. Desalination, and waste water recycling are just 2 of the ways we do it. Then we siphon all the water into a central reservoir for us to utilize. Frequently, local wildlife would wander into our reservoir area  to drink from it. We try to 'educate' the wildlife that the area is out of bounds to them by removing them as soon as possible. Please help us keep the reservoir area clear of any wildlife. The reward is the ensured survival and continued growth of all human kind. A friendly word of warning to you, carrying extra potions and energy canisters might be helpful in this mission.
