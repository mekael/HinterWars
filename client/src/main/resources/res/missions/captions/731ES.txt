After combing every inch of the cave you found a sealed envelope and a few scraps of papers in a hole behind a rock. You start reading the message on the paper ..Finally, back on a Brucian planet. I am so glad to be off the orbiting spaceships, and being pursued. I am so glad to be on solid ground. Being on a Brucian planet makes me think about the times I spent with Calen my dear giant. I remembered the night where he would have his nightmare for the last time. We talked about it, and he finally let go of that hate that he had been bottling up within him all this time. Now that I think about it, I wish I could forgive as easily. I know I would have to face that same darkness that had been haunting me. That day will come when the Aterians find me. I shall leave my last gift to my dearest friend Calen. May you find some use with the contents in the package. Please deliver this package to Major Calen of the Brucian forces. Farewell all! Signed Drey Lum aka Montezuma.
