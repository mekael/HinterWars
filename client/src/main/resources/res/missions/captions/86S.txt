You begin the patrol around the route prescribed for you. All seems quite when some movements in the bush ahead prompts you to dive for cover. You see an unfamiliar figure with 4 legs and 2 arms emerge from the bushes to rejoin the rest of his party some distance ahead and to the left of you. They did not seem to be heavily armed. You start to wonder what and who they were when you felt the cold nozzle of a gun pointed at the back of your neck. Instinctively you raise your hands as a sign of surrender. You didn't dare turn around to look at your assailant for fear of loosing your dear life. But as soon as it became apparent that you might be imprisoned as he started to herd you towards the larger group you reacted. Kill all enemies.
